Security

Data security and sanitization

Your data security is our top priority. We follow industry-leading standards for secure data destruction.

Trusted tools

Secure data destruction using DBAN (Darik's Boot and Nuke) and ShredOS, industry-recognized for thorough sanitization.

Batch-verified

Certified destruction

Process

From pickup to reporting

A clean, verifiable workflow designed for accountability.

1. Intake

Submit your fleet details through our partner intake form. We assess eligibility and provide a quote.

2. Collection

Coordinated pickup with chain of custody tracking across Toronto.

3. Sanitization

Secure data destruction using DBAN or ShredOS, batch-verified and documented.

4. Reporting

Reuse prioritized, e-waste recycled responsibly, and CSR impact reporting delivered.
