Corporate IT Asset Disposition (ITAD)

Certified, secure, and sustainable disposal of your retired IT equipment

Data Security & Sanitization

Your data security is our top priority. We follow industry-leading standards for secure data destruction.

Trusted Open-Source Tools

We perform secure data destruction using trusted open-source tools like DBAN (Darik's Boot and Nuke) and ShredOS. These tools are industry-recognized for thorough data sanitization.

Batch-Automated by Trained Volunteers

Our processes are designed for batch automation, allowing our skilled volunteers to efficiently sanitize large fleets while maintaining the highest security standards.

Certified Destruction

All data destruction activities are documented and can be verified. We provide certificates of data destruction upon request for your compliance records.

CSR & Environmental Reporting

Demonstrate your commitment to sustainability with comprehensive impact reporting.

Environmental Impact Certificates

Corporate donors receive official environmental impact certificates detailing the pounds of e-waste diverted from landfills and the carbon footprint reduction achieved.

CSR Compliance Documentation

Our reporting helps you meet your Corporate Social Responsibility targets with detailed metrics on community impact, waste diversion, and social value creation.

Measurable Impact

Track quantifiable outcomes: devices refurbished and donated, people trained, jobs created in our social enterprise, and environmental metrics.

Understanding Our Model

Clearing up common misconceptions

"Everything at Free Geek is free"

Our reality: While we provide affordable technology and job training to our community, corporate ITAD services have associated costs for secure data destruction, logistics, and processing. Our pricing model is transparent and designed to be sustainable while maximizing our social impact.

"All donations are treated the same"

Our reality: We prioritize corporate bulk submissions of uniform laptops because they optimize our refurbishment efficiency. This allows us to serve more community members with quality devices while keeping our operations lean.

"Free services mean lower quality"

Our reality: We use professional-grade open-source tools (DBAN, ShredOS) and follow strict security protocols. Our trained volunteers and processes meet rigorous standards for corporate ITAD requirements.
