Our Mission

Free Geek Toronto reuses electronic waste to provide affordable technology and job training for underrepresented groups facing barriers to employment. We operate under UN Sustainable Development Goals 8, 10, and 12.

What We Accept

We accept working and repairable computers, laptops, monitors, keyboards, mice, and other computer accessories from individuals.

Donation Process

All individual donations go through our donation intake process. While we welcome individual contributions, corporate bulk donations of uniform laptops receive priority for refurbishment efficiency.

Community Impact

Your individual donation helps provide affordable technology and supports our job training programs for underrepresented groups in Toronto.

Why Choose Free Geek Toronto as Your ITAD Partner

Certified Data Security

We perform secure data destruction using trusted open-source tools like DBAN and ShredOS, batch-automated by our trained volunteers with full audit trails.

CSR & Environmental Reporting

Receive detailed environmental impact certificates and CSR reporting documentation to meet your sustainability goals and compliance requirements.

Flexible Pricing Model

Standard fees for certified data wiping and hardware pickup, but fees are fully waived if you donate high-yield, uniform laptop fleets.

Community Impact

Your retired equipment directly supports affordable technology access and job training for underrepresented groups in Toronto.
